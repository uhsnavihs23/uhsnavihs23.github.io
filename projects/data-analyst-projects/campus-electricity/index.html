Research Context and Objectives

IIT Gandhinagar is committed to building a sustainable and energy efficient campus. As part of this sustainability initiative, it was critical to understand the underlying patterns of electricity consumption across academic blocks, housing grids, and research facilities.

This comprehensive research report transitions raw smart meter data into actionable operational insights. The objective is simple: identify baseline consumption behaviors, isolate peak demand triggers, and formulate practical strategies to optimize energy usage without compromising campus operations.

Optimization Recommendations

Based on comprehensive analysis of 28 months of consumption data

Solar Panel Installation

Install additional solar panels on hostel rooftops

Impact: Potential 3x increase in solar output (7.5 MWh/month)
Payback Period: 5-6 years
Annual Savings: ₹15-20 lakhs annually

AC Temperature Optimization

Set AC to 28°C in hostels, 25-26°C in academic blocks

Impact: 15-20% reduction in cooling consumption
Payback Period: Immediate
Annual Savings: ₹8-12 lakhs annually

Smart Thermostat Implementation

Install programmable thermostats for adaptive control

Impact: 10-15% overall energy savings
Payback Period: 2-3 years
Annual Savings: ₹5-8 lakhs annually

Occupancy-Based Room Design

Prioritize triple rooms over single rooms in new construction

Impact: 25-30% per capita energy reduction
Payback Period: Long-term
Annual Savings: ₹10-15 lakhs annually

Key Findings

  • • Power consumption is projected to double in 6 years with current growth rate
  • • 90% of energy consumption comes from hostels, academic buildings, and chiller plant
  • • Solar contribution averages only 8.5% - significant room for improvement
  • • Peak consumption occurs during October (post-summer break)
