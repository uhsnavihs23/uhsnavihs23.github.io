Key Business Insights

Revenue Leader: Swiggy dominates with 40% market share, generating ₹17.2L in revenue, followed by Zomato at 35% and Blinkit at 25%.
Delivery Efficiency: Average delivery time of 28.5 minutes with Blinkit leading in speed (24.8 min) due to shorter distances for grocery delivery.
Customer Experience: Overall satisfaction score of 4.1/5 with evening orders showing highest satisfaction due to better preparation time.
Weather Impact: Stormy weather increases delivery time by 35%, suggesting need for weather-based surge pricing and resource allocation.
Geographic Performance: Mumbai leads in total revenue (₹8.2L) while Bangalore shows highest average order value (₹487).
Payment Preferences: UPI payments dominate at 40%, followed by card payments at 30%, indicating digital payment adoption in food delivery.
